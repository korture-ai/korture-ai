# Habit map (experiment)

While you work, your AI points out one of your habits in one line, when it shows up. Ask "my habit map" to see them all, with counts and your own words.

We are trying this with a few people. It needs a personal key, and it sends your chats to Korture's cloud only after you say yes. It is not open yet.

[`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) is what the AI follows.
