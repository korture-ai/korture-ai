# Habit map (experiment)

While you work, your AI points out one of your habits in one line, the moment it shows up. Ask "my habit map" to see them all, with counts and your own words.

This is being tried with a few people. It needs a personal key and sends your chats to Korture's cloud only after you say yes. It is not open for sign-up yet.

[`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) is what the AI follows.
