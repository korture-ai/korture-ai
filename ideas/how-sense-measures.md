# How Korture Sense measures

This is the idea behind Korture Sense, in plain words. The questions Sense asks and the rules that turn answers into a number are private. The idea is not.

## The question

People do more and more of their work with AI. From the inside, they cannot tell if they are getting better at it. Sense measures how a person works with AI, so they can see it and improve. It measures the human, never the AI.

## A yardstick from outside you

A sleep score works because sleep science says, from the outside, what good sleep looks like. It never only compares you to yourself.

Our first numbers compared each person to their own average. The best they could say was "different from your usual". That is not a measurement. So Sense scores against a standard from outside the person: what working well with a tool and a team looks like, as other fields have rated it for decades.

## Every message, in its whole chat

Sense reads every message you send, in order, with the whole conversation around it: what came before, the AI's last reply, and the notes the message points to. It never reads a message alone. When nothing was at stake in a message, the honest answer is "not observed", and that is one of the answers.

## How well, not yes or no

Asking "did you check the work?" says little, because almost everyone checks something. Sense asks how well. The middle of the scale is competent work. The top is for getting ahead of a problem before it happens. The bottom is a real miss. 100 means exceptional, and your number is the distance from it.

## The nine moves

| Move | What Sense looks for |
|---|---|
| Brief | How much context you give, and whether it comes before or after things go wrong |
| Hand over | What you give to the AI and what you keep for yourself |
| Plan | How you break the work into steps |
| Steer | How you guide the work while it happens |
| Check | How you check what the AI made |
| Not sure | What you do when you, or the AI, are not sure |
| Correct | How you fix mistakes |
| Reuse | Whether you build ways of working you use again |
| Stop | Whether you know when to stop, or when not to use AI at all |

## Two parts of the number

- **What you did,** 75 of 100: the nine moves.
- **How it felt,** 25 of 100: what the work is doing to you. Frustration at a mistake the AI made costs you nothing.

## A number alone is not enough

Feedback with nothing to do about it can make people worse. So every reading shows the real moments behind it, in your own words, and what people who work well with AI have learned. You choose what to practise: many things, one, or none. The next readings show which way each one went.
